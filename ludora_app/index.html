How to play

  • 🎲 Ludo
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out. Tap a glowing token to move it clockwise.
  • Land on an opponent to send them home (not on ★ or start cells). A 6, a capture or reaching home gives another roll; three 6s forfeit the turn.
  • Exact number needed to enter home. Finish order counts (1st, 2nd, 3rd); the game ends when one player is left.
  • 🥊 Wobble Duel
  • Slide your thumb on the ◀ ▶ pad to run, ⤒ jumps (tap again in the air for a double jump), ⤓ drops through a platform, ⚔️ attacks. Hold the phone upright: players 3–4 on top, 1–2 at the bottom.
  • Every weapon type swings differently: slashes, chops, thrusts, jabs, smashes, spins and sweeps.
  • Grab crates for ❤️ heal, ⚡ speed, 🛡️ shield or 💪 rage. Don't fall off the map! First to 3 rounds wins.
  • 🎭 ইম্পোস্টার (Bangla)
  • Each player secretly sees a word; the imposter gets a similar but different one. Discuss without saying your word, then vote. Caught: everyone else +1. Escaped: imposter +1.
  • 👑 Become the King (Bangla)
  • Exactly 4 players on one phone. Each player secretly picks one folded chit: 👑 Raja 100 · 👮 Police 80 · 🦹 Daku 40 · 🥷 Chor 0. Never tell anyone your role.
  • After all four have looked, Raja reveals himself, then Police must name the Chor. Right guess: Police 80, Chor 0. Wrong guess: Police 0 and the Chor takes 80. Raja and Daku always keep 100 and 40.
  • Choose how many rounds to play. The highest total at the end becomes the King!
  • ♞ Chess
  • Tap a piece, then tap a highlighted square to move. Castling, en passant and promotion are all supported.
  • Play a friend on one phone (fixed board, flip each turn, or face to face) or the computer on 8 levels from Noob to Grandmaster. Optional clock, undo and hints against the computer.
